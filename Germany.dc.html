Common questions

Do I need to already speak German?

No. Training starts from A1, assuming no prior German knowledge, and progresses through to B2.

Will my nursing qualification be recognised in Germany?

Our RegCheck Compliance Service guides you through the Anerkennung recognition process required by German authorities.

Who handles my visa and travel?

VisaPro Filing Service and Relocate360 Settlement support you through visa filing, travel, and initial settling-in arrangements.

Does support end once I land in Germany?

No. OnboardPro, CultureConnect, and MentorLink continue supporting your integration and career progression after arrival.
