Vani Rastogi

Director, NexaCare Talent Mobility

With over a decade of experience in the staffing and recruitment industry, Vani Rastogi is an accomplished recruitment and business development professional with expertise in client relationship management, talent acquisition, team leadership, and HR strategy. During her tenure as Vice President at HiringHut Solutions, Vani played a key role in driving business growth, building strong client relationships, acquiring new clients, and leading recruitment teams. Her strategic approach to staffing and business development contributed significantly to the company's growth and financial success.

As Director of NexaCare Talent Mobility, Vani brings this extensive industry experience to the healthcare talent sector. She is focused on building a trusted platform that connects qualified nurses, doctors, and healthcare professionals with rewarding career opportunities in India and international markets.

Her leadership is driven by a commitment to professional recruitment, candidate success, strong industry partnerships, and ethical talent mobility. With a deep understanding of the staffing industry and a people-first approach, Vani plays a key role in shaping the company's vision and expanding its presence in the global healthcare recruitment market.

Syed Kamran

Director, NexaCare Talent Mobility

With nearly two decades of experience in recruitment, talent acquisition, client relationship management, and business development, Syed Kamran brings extensive industry expertise and a strong strategic vision to NexaCare Talent Mobility. As Director, he is focused on building a trusted global healthcare talent ecosystem that connects qualified nurses, doctors, and healthcare professionals with leading career opportunities across India and international markets.

Throughout his career, Syed Kamran has developed strong expertise in understanding workforce requirements, building long-term client relationships, developing effective recruitment strategies, and delivering talent solutions tailored to evolving business needs.

His leadership focuses on global healthcare recruitment, talent mobility, strategic partnerships, and sustainable business growth. He is committed to creating meaningful opportunities for healthcare professionals while helping organizations access skilled and qualified talent. With a combination of extensive recruitment experience, strategic leadership, and a relationship-driven approach, Syed Kamran plays an important role in shaping the company's vision of becoming a trusted partner in global healthcare talent mobility.
