About NexaCare

NexaCare is an international healthcare career solutions company based in Bengaluru, India, specializing in preparing and placing skilled healthcare professionals across India and global markets.

We work closely with recruitment agencies, hospitals, healthcare groups, and educational institutions to build a reliable pipeline of qualified candidates for both domestic and international employment opportunities.
